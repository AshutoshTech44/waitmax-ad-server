Privacy Policy — WaitMax AI

100% Zero-Chat Privacy Verified   Effective Date: November 18, 2026

WaitMax AI (Universal AI Wait-Time Companion) respects your privacy. This policy explains clearly what data is processed and what is strictly never touched.

1. Zero-Chat & Zero-Prompt Guarantee

  • No Content Reading: WaitMax AI NEVER reads, scrapes, logs, stores, or transmits your AI prompts, chat messages, uploaded documents, images, or AI-generated outputs.
  • No Browsing History Tracking: The extension operates only on supported AI tool interfaces when a generation process is active.

2. Limited Technical Data Used

  • Anonymous Device UUID (waitmax_uuid): Stored locally in chrome.storage.local to sync your WaitCoins reward balance with our server (waitmax-ad-server.vercel.app). No name, email, or password is required.
  • UI Generation State Only: Detects standard Stop or Generating interface buttons solely to display optional, clearly labeled SPONSORED cards during wait times.
  • Aggregated Ad Metrics: Anonymous view and click counts are recorded to calculate creator and user reward pools.

3. User Control, Opt-In Consent & Data Deletion

  • Explicit Opt-In: Sponsored cards are shown only with your active consent.
  • Instant Pause or Close: You can close any card anytime via the ✕ button or disable ads from the extension popup.
  • Data Removal: Uninstalling the extension immediately deletes all local storage data.
